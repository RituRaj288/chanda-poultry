Institutional Profile

About CHANDA POULTRY AND SILAGE FEEDS PRIVATE LIMITED

Dedicated to elevating commercial poultry nutrition, year-round fermented Napier grass silage feed supply, and B2B enterprise performance across Haryana and North India.

Who We Are

Purpose-Built for Modern Agri-Enterprise & Nutritional Excellence

CHANDA POULTRY AND SILAGE FEEDS PRIVATE LIMITED (operated with NR Entrepreneur — Divya Beniwal) is headquartered at Chaki Chopta Mor, Mandi Adampur, Distt. Hisar, Haryana, with its Corporate Office at MLA Flat Society No. 1, 2nd Block, Sector 24, Panchkula, Haryana – 134116.

Our enterprise unites two specialized flagship divisions: CHANDA POULTRY FEED (Mandi Adampur)—formulating high-energy yellow maize and roasted soybean rations for chicks, broilers, and layers—and CHANDA SILAGE (Napier Grass Feed)—producing anaerobically fermented, high-density UV-wrapped green forage bales for dairy cows and Murrah buffaloes.

Alongside our core livestock and poultry nutrition divisions, we partner with commercial farms, wholesale mandi traders, FPOs, and growing enterprises through structured B2B supply chain coordination and operational advisory.

Dual-Hub Operations: Hisar & Panchkula

By combining direct agricultural sourcing and production dispatch at Mandi Adampur (Distt. Hisar) with responsive corporate management in Sector 24, Panchkula, we ensure dependable batch quality and seamless regional logistics.

Working Methodology

Structured, Auditable, and Outcome-Oriented

Our methodology eliminates guesswork across both physical feed production and corporate B2B supply:

    Baseline Requirement & Ration Alignment Documenting exact nutritional targets, flock/herd stage, and order volumes before dispatch.
